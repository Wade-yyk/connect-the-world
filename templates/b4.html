I use four-point RANSAC on the B.3 matches. I repeatedly sample four pairs, calculate H, and count matches with a reprojection error below $threshold px, where error = ||position predicted by H − B.3 matched position||2. I skip degenerate samples, keep the largest consensus, and refit H using its inliers. The automatic H matrices use only automatically matched points. Then I use the method in A.4 to merge the pictures.

RANSAC rejects inconsistent matches, but a single H still cannot align every depth when the photos contain parallax. For example, the roof edge is still misaligned.
